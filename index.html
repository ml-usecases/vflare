Private budget tool

Transaction Checker

Transaction Checker is a private, owner-operated tool for reviewing bank transactions and maintaining a budget workbook. It reads bank CSV exports and categorization rules from a configured Google Drive folder and Google Sheet, refreshes the workbook, and prepares reports of transactions that need review.

The scheduled workflow can also retrieve transactions from the owner's Starling account to update the budget and check transfers. It sends a generic email when new items need review; the message does not contain transaction details and links to the reports in Google Drive.

This is not a public account service. It has no public registration or user dashboard. Access is authorized by the owner through Google OAuth.
